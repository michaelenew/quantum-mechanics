## The common core

Both programs are the same object seen from two ends: **an ensemble

carried separately from state.** The physics side has exactness and
symmetry structure but no arrow of time and no operational loss. The
filter side has causality, data, and prequential scoring but no
geometry. Each side's native strength covers the other's blind spot.

## The adoption plan

**F1 — The vertex propagator as a coupled-bank transfer function.**
*Physics problem*: the boundary-state vertex + momentum propagator
(the crossing proper). *Filter formulation*: two lucid filters
sharing their process-scale posterior — the shared node IS the
vertex; the propagator is the **cross-stream transfer function**: how
an innovation in stream 1 moves predictions in stream 2 through the
shared posterior. Masslessness ↔ long-memory (1/f) cross-response.
*Minimal extension*: a two-stream WalkingBank with one shared scale
channel; measure the cross-transfer and its low-frequency limit.
*Ports back*: which shared-parameterizations keep the cross-response
long-range — and the oracle-gap **decomposition method** (their
80/9.5/6.8/3.7 accounting) applied to the propagator: forced /
collapse / model / lag channels of the vertex. *Trigger*: when the
boundary-state computation stalls on intertwiner combinatorics.

**F2 — The running of τ as prequential regret growth.** *Physics
problem*: marginal vs slowly confining — MK cannot resolve the logs.
*Filter formulation*: parametric log-loss regret grows as
(k/2)·log T — **a running logarithm is the filter's native
object**, and prequential scoring measures it exactly, no MK
analogue needed. *Minimal extension*: temporal decimation cascade on
the shipped WalkingFilter — block the stream by 2 repeatedly, refit,
track the flow of the fitted forgetting/precision coordinates: the
filter's own MK flow with an exact instrument. *Ports back*: whether
an information-derived weight family is RG-stable or log-drifts,
measured rather than approximated. *Trigger*: **filter-first** — this
row is plausibly *stronger* than both the full theory and the Z_N
pivot, because the instrument is exact.

**F3 — The Lorentzian lift as causal attainability.** *Physics
problem*: the quantum arc is Euclidean. *Filter formulation*: the
filter is already Lorentzian — causal, online, time distinguished.
Euclidean ↔ batch/smoothing; Lorentzian ↔ filtering. The lift
becomes: **which parts of the batch (smoothed) posterior are causally
attainable?** — and the filter has already measured its causal
ceiling (96.3% of oracle). *Minimal extension*: bank-level
smoother-vs-filter comparison: which posterior structures survive
the causal restriction, which are irreducibly two-sided. *Ports
back*: a template for what survives the Euclidean → Lorentzian
passage, organized by the oracle-gap decomposition. *Trigger*: when
the Lorentzian lift of the measure needs more than the ⋆² = −1
criterion (already proven over Q, 0063).

**F4 — The Born structure's utility as coherent IMM.** *Physics
problem*: the sign problem's cure (Born square) awaits re-proof at
the boundary vertex; deeper, we know the square is *necessary* but
not what it *buys*. *Filter formulation*: IMM mixes probabilities;
a coherent bank carries amplitudes with interference and squares at
readout. If amplitude-mixing measurably beats probability-mixing on
any classical tracking task (oscillatory/multimodal latents), the
filter discovers the Born structure's utility from pure information
dynamics — the opposite-side derivation of the ledger. *Minimal
extension*: two-node coherent bank on a switching sinusoid, scored
in code length against IMM. *Ports back*: an operational reason for
the square, and possibly the positivity principle the vertex re-proof
needs. *Trigger*: when the vertex positivity re-proof stalls — or
simply when curiosity wins; this is the highest-upside row.

**F5 — The tangle as cross-spectral tracking.** *Physics problem*:
relational records (0067: ρ_A is θ-independent — the coordinate
lives only in the pair) and the matter thread's thinness. *Filter
formulation*: a parameter invisible in every marginal stream,
carried only in the cross-correlation — the filter version of
entanglement. *Minimal extension*: two streams individually
parameter-free whose joint correlation carries the signal; the bank
that tracks it. CKW monogamy becomes testable as a **cross-
information budget** across stream pairs. *Ports back*: the
information-theoretic content of monogamy with an operational loss
attached. *Trigger*: when the matter/relational thread reopens.

**F6 — The budget and Λ as the unobservable common mode.** *Physics
problem*: the zero mode / closed-universe budget / Λ residual (the
0069 falsifiability route). *Filter formulation*: the level a
differencing filter cannot see — (1−B)^ν literally removes it, and
the fractional family is a *continuum of how much zero mode to
remove*; the Λ-residual is the drift the differencing leaves.
*Minimal extension*: the ν-family's treatment of a slow common mode
across a bank — what residual is identifiable. *Ports back*: the
budget's boundary form (Λ1) seen from the causal side. *Trigger*:
when Λ1 (budget with boundary) runs.
