porting.** Their epistricted result is a *scope theorem this program

**2. Two streams, one door at 2.** The arithmetic branch: binary
fibers cannot braid (S₂ is abelian); noncommutativity — and with it

doubled. This stream, independently and in a different formalism:

(0090). The knowledge frame and the arithmetic ledger both found
that *two is degenerate and doubling opens the door* — from opposite
sides, neither aware of the other's form of it. By the program's own
standards (unforced coherence across formalisms) this is the pass's
strongest evidence that the transfer lost nothing essential: the
structures reconverge.
