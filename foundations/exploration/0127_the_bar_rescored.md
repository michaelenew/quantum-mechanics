## 2. Something the bar did not ask for

0069's table lists what each program *assumes*: string theory
assumes QM; LQG assumes GR + quantization rules; asymptotic safety
assumes a QFT of the metric. The bar therefore contains no
requirement "derive quantum mechanics" — nobody attempts it.

This program now does. The chain closed as of 0126: the record's
content is *interference* (lucid 0033); segments compose by
**complex multiplication** because two ledgers add and the record is
order-blind (lucid 0034); the weight is a **square** because the
budget fixes the band and the band fixes the degree (0119, 0126);
alternatives are **summed** because degree 2 *is* the sum rule
(0126, lucid 0035); and the Lorentzian arena follows from counting
via reflection positivity (0123).

**Scored honestly, that is over-delivery on an axis the bar did not
measure** — and it does not substitute for (D). A derivation of the
quantum tier makes the postulates look inevitable; it is not
evidence that the postulates are true.
