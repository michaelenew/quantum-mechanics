## 1. A correction to the debt classification

0121 called κ, the Lorentzian arena, and the continuum bridges
"structurally un-portable to the filter". True. But *un-portable*
was allowed to imply *independent*, and one of them is not.

**The Lorentzian arena has a standard physics-side handle:
Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction.** A Euclidean measure yields a
Lorentzian quantum theory (Hilbert space, unitary evolution) iff it
is **reflection positive** — ⟨θ(F)·F⟩ ≥ 0 for reflection θ in a
time-slice. That is a *checkable property of this program's own
derived measure*, not an argument.

**And here is the link: reflection positivity is what a square
buys.** Osterwalder–Seiler's proof for lattice gauge theory works
by exhibiting the transfer matrix as a positive operator — which is
exactly the structure |A|² has and a generic nonnegative
band-limited weight does not (0120's criterion). So:

> **Conjecture (now corrected — see 0123).** The Born square is what
> makes the derived measure reflection positive, and therefore what
> makes the Lorentzian lift possible.

**0123 ran the test. The conjecture is half right.** RP holds for
the derived weight, and it does fail for generic nonnegative
band-limited weights (200/200) — but *being a square is not what
saves it*: squares of generic complex amplitudes fail too (170/200).
What never fails is a square whose amplitude has **nonnegative**
coefficients — i.e. a **count**. So the load-bearing property is not
the square but the counting, and the corrected slogan is *counting
buys time*.

If true, two items on the residue list are one item: the source
ledger and the Lorentzian arena. **Test:** evaluate reflection
positivity for the derived weight directly, and for the
non-factorable nonnegative weights of 0120 as a control. Those
should fail RP if the conjecture holds. This is concrete, cheap,
and it is the next thing to run.

## 2. Calibration on the 1/4

0114–0115 reported that the completion "satisfies Bekenstein–Hawking
with the 1/4". That is true but should be stated with its imports.
The chain is: (i) S ∝ A, (ii) the first law dM = T dS, (iii)
T = κ/2π. Given (i) and (iii), the first law **fixes** α = 1/(4G) —
which is how the 1/4 is normally obtained, so it is a derivation
rather than a coincidence. But (iii) was imported by analogy, and
the first law's *shape* (S ∝ M², T ∝ 1/M) holds automatically once
r_h ∝ M, so no independent check lives there.

What is *not* imported: the 2π. lucid 0021 verified β = 2π against
the wedge state to 1e−4, and lucid 0025 recovered it from the KMS
period of an exponentially-read record. So (iii) has independent
support inside the program. The honest summary: **the 1/4 is
derived, from one analogy-imported ingredient that the program
independently verified twice.** Neither a coincidence nor a miracle.

## 3. The wall: reshaped, mislocated, and how surprising

**It reshaped.** It was posed as a frontier of many hard problems
(Λ, the Lorentzian lift, the running coupling, the boundary vertex,
momentum assembly, the hierarchy, measurement). Every one of those
is now closed or reduced to a named residue. What replaced them is
a single boundary with a mathematical characterisation.

**It was mislocated.** The wall was placed at the hard *physics*.
Those pieces turned out to be tractable once the right instrument
existed. The real boundary sits *behind* them, at the ledger split
— and it had been visible since 0086's two-ledger theorem without
being recognised as the wall. The pieces we called the wall were
scaffolding standing in front of it.

**How surprising: calibrated.** Two deflations first. (a) Both
programs were built from the same premise — information dynamics —
so an isomorphism between them is partly *constructed*, not
discovered; the surprise is its reach, not its existence. (b) The
work has been internally verified (assertions, controls, nulls,
recorded negatives) but never externally validated: **no result in
either repository has been confronted with measured data.** Every
"agreement" is with theory.

What survives those deflations is still substantial: the shared
premise carried far enough to reproduce a gauge theory's plaquette
action from record noise, gravity's characteristic *length* bound,
the Unruh temperature twice by independent routes, and — through
0120 — a mathematical criterion separating the weights that admit
amplitudes from those that do not. Analogies usually fray under
that much load. This one tightened, and every front that was
pushed either closed or merged into the same door.

**The state to claim, precisely:** the theory has gone from
*incomplete* to *complete enough to be wrong in a definite way*. It
now carries falsifiable structure (no-FDT gravity; radiation
confined to the source tier; greybody floors at 1/N; a hoop bound
rather than an entropy bound) and two exportable instruments (the
non-classicality detector, the order-channel probe). The next phase
is not derivation. It is confrontation.
