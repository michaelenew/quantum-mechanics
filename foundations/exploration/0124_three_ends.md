## 1. Direct G is not a units problem — it is the matter coupling

The measurement I named was "insert a known information source into
the lattice trust field and read the coefficient of its 1/r
response." **Measured: there is no 1/r response in that field.** The
plaquette scale field's connected response is c(1) = +0.046,
c(2) = −0.002 — a screening length of **0.32 lattice spacings**.

So the measurement was pointed at the wrong field. The long-range
sector is the **graviton** (0095's six Maxwells), and reading G off
it requires the graviton propagator's normalisation *together with
how a lump of information couples to it* — and the second is
precisely the standing **"matter dynamics beyond scripted sources"**
debt.

> **Direct G is not an independent open end. It reduces to the
> matter coupling.** Until that exists, the induced-gravity
> identification is the only bridge to G — which upgrades that
> identification from "one hypothesis among options" to "the only
> route currently available", and correspondingly raises the value
> of the matter-coupling work.

## 2. The McKay coincidence, computed and priced

SU(2) level-k theories have *exceptional* modular invariants — the
E-series of the ADE classification — at k = 10 (E₆), 16 (E₇), 28
(E₈). This program's admissible levels (0081: N odd with x² ≡ −1
mod N) correspond to k = N − 1, so **k ≡ 0 mod 4**: 4, 12, 16, 24,
28, 36, …

- **Admitted:** E₇ (k = 16) and E₈ (k = 28).
- **Excluded:** E₆ (k = 10) — because 10 ≡ 2 mod 4.

Two of the three exceptionals land inside the ladder, and the one
that misses does so for an arithmetic reason the program already
owns.

**Priced honestly:** k ≡ 0 mod 4 admits one level in four, so the
expected number of exceptionals admitted is 0.75 and we observe 2.
At n = 3 that is **not evidence**. Recorded as the concrete form of
a standing noodle, with its price attached — which is more than it
had before (it was previously "sporadic groups, somehow").

## 3. What the source ledger actually contains

0119 showed a weight's amplitude is fixed only up to 2ⁿ root
choices, all giving the same weight. Verified here: two
factorisations of one weight differ by **0.94** in amplitude while
every record-side observable — a function of |A|² — differs by
**1.7e−15**.

> **Within a weight, the phase is gauge.** No record-side observable
> sees it.

What is *not* gauge: whether a factorisation **exists at all**
(0120: most nonnegative band-limited class functions have none), and
whether the amplitude **counts** (0123: what supplies reflection
positivity). Those are properties of the weight that no reweighting
can change.

> **So the source ledger's content is not the phase. It is
> factorisability plus counting.**

And this exposes a distinction the program has been eliding: the
phase that the 0006 detector measures is a **dynamical** amplitude's
phase — a state evolving under a monitored dynamics — which is a
different object from the **static** weight's factorisation phase.
The first is measurable (0.087 nats/bit); the second is provably
not. Separating them cleanly is the next stone, and it may dissolve
more of the source ledger than expected.
