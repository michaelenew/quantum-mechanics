A classification stone, no module. 0048's honest residue has been
carried as "physics-internal debts with no filter doppelganger".
Examining each from first principles shows that phrase was doing too
much work: **two items were misclassified**, three are structurally
un-portable for identifiable reasons, and three sit behind the one
remaining wall.

## The criterion that emerges

A filter corollary exists exactly when a question is about
**inference, information flow, or model selection**. It fails in
exactly four situations:

1. **Absolute units.** The filter has one ledger — nats — and no
   external ruler. It fixes *ratios* (G·g², ℓ_P/a) and never
   absolutes.
2. **The arena it presupposes.** A filter is *given* an ordered
   record. It cannot derive the existence of causal order without
   circularity.
3. **The continuum.** The filter is inherently discrete; its only
   limit is infinite *data*, which is a statistical limit, not a
   geometric one.
4. **The source ledger.** Established repeatedly (0120, lucid
   0005/0024/0027): record-side inference cannot reach amplitude
   structure.

## The debts, classified

**Class A — structurally un-portable (1–3 above)**
- **κ normalisation.** The conversion between nats and geometric
  units. The filter's geometry *is* its information geometry, so
  the conversion is 1 by construction; it cannot produce a number
  relating nats to lengths because lengths do not exist in it.
  Reason 1. (This is why the induced-gravity route — comparing two
  *physics-side* quantities — is the only handle, and why lucid
  0029's G·g² is a ratio.)
- **The Lorentzian arena.** The Euclidean-web → Lorentzian-spacetime
  step. The filter has given us a great deal here (0103: Euclidean =
  smoother; 0016: what causality costs; lucid 0028: stationarity
  selects the boost orbit) — but every one of those *starts* from an
  ordered stream. Reason 2: deriving the arena from a machine that
  presupposes it is circular.
- **Arithmetic → continuum bridges.** 0010's finite-frame results in
  the continuum. Reason 3.

**Class B — behind the source ledger (4 above)**
- **P4 → Tsirelson.** Not a separate debt: it is the source-ledger
  door seen from the correlations side. lucid 0024 showed classical
  information copies freely (no bound) while monogamy is an
  amplitude property; a bound of Tsirelson's kind can only come from
  the same structure whose non-derivability 0120 established.
- **Matter dynamics beyond scripted sources; the frame field's full
  quantum dynamics.** Both require the wave/source sector that lucid
  0029 showed the record tier does not possess.
- **The two-body residual**, partly: separating O(v) from O(h²)
  needs genuine dynamics of moving sources, which is source-tier;
  the rest is their perturbation bookkeeping.

**Class C — misclassified (corrections)**
- **Lattice Palatini.** *A corollary exists and is already built.*
  The Palatini question is "is the connection independent of the
  metric, with torsion-freedom an equation of motion?" — which is
  exactly the filter's "is the transport inferred jointly with the
  state?" lucid 0026–0027 answered it: the connection **is** an
  inferred nuisance parameter, and its action is the code length of
  frame records. Torsion-freedom is that action's stationarity
  condition. What remains on the physics side is the lattice algebra
  (e, B = e∧e, the torsion equation on 0030's lattice) — **labour,
  not obstruction**, and it now has a filter-side statement to
  match against.
- **Content and rung.** Not a gap but a *category*: 0106 priced the
  level as a measured constant (58 samples) and 0120 showed neither
  the arithmetic ladder nor the budget selects it. The filter's
  epistemology classifies these correctly as world-data. **Closed in
  status.**
- **The closed-loop test.** A measured artefact of open ends in
  their own construction (0047), needing a compact conserved string.
  A technical fix, not a conceptual gap; a filter corollary would
  test a different system's conservation and answer a different
  question.

## What this leaves

Of nine standing items: three are structurally un-portable for
stated reasons, three are the source ledger under other names, two
are labour with the conceptual work already done, and one is a
category classification that is already correct. **The residue is
not a list of nine problems; it is one wall, two chores, and three
statements about what a filter is.**
