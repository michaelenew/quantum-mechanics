## Path A — the wall (interacting continuous quantum measure)

**Straight line:**

- **A0 — the exact RG probe of the derived measure** (cheapest
  decisive step; *executed in 0071*). The ledger weight has no
  coupling knob — it is derived — so its phase is a fact, not a
  choice. In the abelian sector that fact is computable exactly:
  2D by explicit blocking (dual weight → 4th power, divisor family
  closed), 3D by Wegner duality to spin models, 4D by self-duality.
  Done-criterion: know which phase the ledger sits in, per dimension
  and level.
- **A1 — the continuum frame kernel.** Redo 0053/0061's Gauss sum
  with frames in ℝ⁴ instead of Z_N⁴: K(F) = ∫da db e^{iεabF} is an
  oscillatory integral computable by rank — expected: a delta on
  simple bivectors plus a 1/Pf² tail (Barrett–Crane's delta *plus a
  derived correction tail*, where BC's pathology was the bare
  delta). Done-criterion: closed form by rank, regularization
  understood (compact frames ⇒ smeared delta).
- **A2 — the one-vertex nonabelian model.** SU(2) links on a minimal
  complex with the A1 weight, character expansion truncated at
  j_max, exact partial sums. Done-criterion: convergent geometric
  expectations on one vertex.
- **A3 — the graviton propagator test.** Two-point curvature
  correlation on a minimal complex vs 0063's free graviton at weak
  coupling. This is exactly where Barrett–Crane bled (wrong
  propagator asymptotics) and EPRL survived. Done-criterion: match
  or a diagnosed mismatch.

**Then do:** blocking step for the nonabelian weight (is the derived
family RG-closed as the divisor ensemble was?); SL(2,C) reality
conditions; sources on the complex; the marriage with 0063's mode
count (deconfined substrate + continuous modes = gravitons).

**Alternatives:** (i) Hamiltonian route — transfer matrices and the
0056-style pair spectra, exactly diagonalizable in small sectors,
sidestepping the Euclidean measure; (ii) adopt the EPRL vertex
wholesale and test *our* observables (deficit law, tangle
discriminator) inside it — fastest contact, least ours; (iii)
organize by strong-coupling/character expansion rather than weak.

## Path C — semiclassics (Hawking/Unruh/area laws)

**Straight line:**

- **C1 — graviton half-space entanglement.** 0063's free lattice
  gravitons are Gaussian; the entanglement of the zero-point state
  across a spatial cut is exact covariance-matrix algebra. Expected:
  an area law with a computable coefficient. Done-criterion: the
  area law measured, coefficient extracted.
- **C2 — thermality.** The reduced half-space state's modular
  structure vs the boost (Bisognano–Wichmann on the lattice, still
  Gaussian). Done-criterion: thermal form confirmed/refuted with a
  temperature.
- **C3 — entropy = lost pairwise capacity.** The web-native
  derivation: cut the web, count the capacity (tangle + coherence,
  per 0068) crossing the cut; tie to the deficit law and monogamy.
  Done-criterion: cut entropy ∝ area with the coefficient expressed
  in ledger units.
- **C4 — the 1/4.** Confront C1/C3's coefficient with A/4G under the
  κ-is-a-unit reading (0049). The 1/4 is the hard part; treat a
  mismatch as a finding, not a failure.

**Then do:** the saturated-channel conjecture — 0065's m =
(1−e^{−I})/4G says I → ∞ drives a channel to the extremal defect:
*is a saturated channel a horizon?* If yes, Hawking-adjacent
statements become channel statements.

**Alternatives:** topological entanglement entropy of the 4D
deconfined ledger phase (ties directly to 0071's finding); Unruh
proper requires matter — defer to (E).

## Path N/Λ — a derived number

**Straight line (Λ route, from 0069):**

- **Λ1 — the budget with boundary.** Redo 0053's link integration on
  an *open* Z_N lattice: the closed-universe budget Σ F = 0 should
  become Σ F = boundary holonomy, exactly. Cheap and decisive for
  the mechanism. Done-criterion: the boundary form of the budget,
  exact.
- **Λ2 — the zero mode in the τ theory** (0064 §4's delicacy,
  resolved rather than noted).
- **Λ3 — the residual spectrum**: allowed total curvature in units
  2πn/N on closed arenas.
- **Λ4 — the confrontation**: Λ_obs·(horizon volume) vs the quantum;
  the N it demands vs the N the hierarchy demands. Two
  knob-derivations meeting at one value = the program's first
  prediction.

**Then do:** the cosmological arena (needs F, the arena work).

**Alternatives:** N from P4/Tsirelson (long shot, dormant); t's
renormalization via A3 (overlaps path A); and note that **0071
already delivers the first derived constraint on N**: rigidity of 4D
geometry requires N ≥ 3.
