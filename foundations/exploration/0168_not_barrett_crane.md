## The prior question

Whether a lattice gauge theory is intertwiner-blind is **not a choice
made in the weight**. Expand Z in characters and the link integrals
appear on their own:

    ∫ dU  ⊗_i D^{j_i}(U)

Haar integration projects onto the invariant subspace, and in four
dimensions each link is shared by **four** plaquettes — a 4-valent
node. So the question is settled by the **rank of that projector**.

**Gate first** (a first attempt had the symmetric-power indexing
backwards — the convolution tracks the second basis power, not the
first):

| check | result |
|---|---|
| `D(A)D(B) = D(AB)` | 2.99e−16 |
| `D†D = I` | 5.55e−16 |

## The measurement

| four faces at a link | rank of projector | dim Inv |
|---|---|---|
| (½,½,½,½) | **2** | 2 |
| (1,1,1,1) | **3** | 3 |
| (½,½,1,1) | **2** | 2 |
| (1,1,1,2) | **2** | 2 |

> **The rank is dim Inv, not 1.** Haar integration over a link retains
> the entire intertwiner space. Nothing in the face weight can remove
> it — the projector is generated by the **measure**, not chosen by
> the amplitude.

For the Spin(4) double copy the balanced condition ties j⁺ = j⁻ *face
by face*, and the link integral factorises over SU(2)⁺ × SU(2)⁻, so
the surviving space is Inv⁺ ⊗ Inv⁻ — **36 intertwiner states per link**
at the band limit's top spin.

**Barrett–Crane does something this does not.** Its vertex
*additionally* projects that space onto a single element — the
Barrett–Crane intertwiner — which is what makes its amplitude depend
on face labels alone, and what Alesci and Rovelli traced the failure
to. This construction imposes simplicity at the **face** level and
imposes **nothing** at the node.

## What the filter contributed

lucid 0051 supplied the meaning and the price. An intertwiner is the
**shape** of a closed node modulo rotation: dimension 2n − 6, measured
0, 2, 4, 6 for n = 3…6. At 3 valence the shape is a point — magnitudes
determine everything. At 4 it is two-dimensional, which is exactly why
a tetrahedron's face areas do not fix its geometry. Discarding it
costs up to **ln 6 = 1.79 nats per node** here.

And it made the argument the program could not make from inside: the
capacity principle **counts states**, so applied at a node it *forbids*
the blind form rather than forcing it. κ is untouched either way,
because κ lives on face labels and the intertwiner lives at nodes.

That prediction was never tested — the port found the premise false
first.

## What this does and does not settle

**Settles:** the specific reason to expect this program's graviton
propagator to be wrong is **removed**. 0166's flag was based on a
structural identification that does not survive the measurement, and
0167's "most serious open item" is retired.

**Does not settle:** the graviton propagator has still never been
measured here. Removing a reason to expect failure is not evidence of
success.

**And one residual, stated so it is not lost.** This construction
imposes simplicity *strongly* at the face level (j⁺ = j⁻ exactly),
where EPRL/FK impose it *weakly*. Strong imposition is part of what
the spin foam literature holds against Barrett–Crane, independently of
the intertwiner question. So the honest placement is: **neither BC nor
EPRL — strong simplicity on faces, free intertwiners at nodes.** That
combination is not obviously either model's, and whether it is
consistent is not established here.
