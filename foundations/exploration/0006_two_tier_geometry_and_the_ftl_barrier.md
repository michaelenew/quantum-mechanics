Taking up the user's observation that relativity does not axiomatically
forbid faster-than-light — it forbids **continuous crossing of c**
(infinite-energy barrier). This is exactly the loophole quantum "collapse"
fits through, and the two-tier structure (`0003`) has a clean geometric
reading in these terms.

## What SR actually forbids

The strict content of relativistic causality:

1. **No causal influence propagates along a timelike/null curve with `v > c`.**
   A continuous worldline cannot cross the light cone.
2. **No usable signal reaches spacelike-separated events.** ("No-signalling"
   — weaker than 1, and this is what QM respects.)

SR does **not** forbid:

- Spacelike-separated events being *correlated* (they always are, if they
  share a past cause).
- Discrete, instantaneous events that are not continuous worldlines. There is
  no continuous trajectory here to run afoul of the infinite-energy barrier.
- Structures (constraints, gauge-fixings, coordinate choices) defined on
  spacelike slices as a whole.

The tachyon literature is a distraction: tachyons are dynamical particles with
imaginary rest mass, and field-theoretically they signal vacuum instability,
not FTL communication. We do **not** need tachyons for the two-tier picture.
The cleaner slot is the one the user identified — **discontinuous events**
that are not continuous crossings.

## The two-tier structure, geometrically

- **Actionable knowledge = dynamics on the manifold.** Propagates along
  timelike/null curves. Continuous evolution. Bounded by `c`. This is
  everything covered by Hamiltonian evolution / effective field theory /
  classical forces.
- **Correlational knowledge = structure of the manifold.** Lives on spacelike
  slices as the consistency data of the web. Not a propagation, so no speed
  applies. Discrete updates when a coincidence event sharpens an edge; the
  spacelike slice re-shapes to satisfy the constraint.

The distant, "instantaneous" edge update in an EPR scenario is not a signal
crossing space — it is the re-slicing of the manifold to keep the constraint
satisfied. Nothing crosses `c` because nothing crosses at all. The
randomness firewall (`foundations/0003`) is precisely the "no-usable-signal"
half of relativistic causality; the two-tier structure is what makes both
halves compatible.

## Why this is not a metaphysical dodge

Two things make the two-tier picture more than a semantic move:

1. **It is standard mathematical structure.** A Lorentzian manifold has both
   causal (light-cone) and Riemannian-on-slices structure. Constraint
   surfaces (in gauge theory, in the ADM formulation of GR, in canonical
   quantization) live on spacelike slices and are re-satisfied when a local
   change happens. This is not an invention — it's how modern physics is
   already formulated.
2. **It predicts something concrete.** Anything that requires *continuous*
   propagation of information (forces, gradients, controllable signals) is
   `c`-bounded. Anything that is a *discrete constraint update* is not — and,
   crucially, **cannot be used to signal**, because the update is random per
   coincidence and averages away in distant marginals.

## Testable content of the partition

- Every observed FTL constraint is on **continuous** propagation:
  light-speed cap on light, group velocity of matter, no-signalling in EPR.
  ✓ consistent.
- Every observed "instantaneous" effect is **discrete + non-signalling**:
  wave-function collapse, EPR correlations, entanglement swapping.
  ✓ consistent.
- The theory says nothing controversial that experiment already contradicts.

## Where this framing bites (what it changes)

Two things it earns beyond the interpretational.

1. **It removes the alleged conflict.** "QM has instantaneous collapse and
   also respects relativity" is often taught as a puzzle to be papered over
   by decoherence or many-worlds. The two-tier picture says the two things
   describe different geometric objects — dynamics on the manifold vs.
   structure of the manifold. There is no conflict, only a category error.
2. **It suggests the right variables for engineering.** If a phenomenon is
   about continuous propagation of information — use effective Hamiltonians,
   Lorentz-covariant fields, wave equations. If it is about discrete
   constraint satisfaction — use the constraint-manifold projection (MRE
   update; the Bures/Fisher geometry of `0005`). Choosing the wrong tool for
   the wrong tier is why several standard problems (quantum measurement,
   apparent nonlocality) *feel* paradoxical.
