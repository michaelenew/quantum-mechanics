## 1. The bar, requirement by requirement

What the field demands of a "quantum theory of gravity," and where
this program honestly stands:

**(A) Quantum states of geometry itself, with interacting dynamics.**
Superpositions of geometries evolving consistently — not just a
quantized weak field. *Status: partial at the toy tier.* The Z_N arc
genuinely superposes geometries (the deficit is an expectation value,
the jitter is real interference), but those are defect geometries
with a finiteness ceiling (0061 §4). The continuous theory is
quantized only at the free/linearized level (0063). **The wall is
the interacting, continuous, 3+1 quantum measure.** This is the same
wall every program hits — perturbative nonrenormalizability *is* the
statement that the naive crossing fails. We have not failed at this
wall yet because we have not attempted the crossing. The one asset
nobody else brings to it: a measure *derived* from postulates (0064's
Dirichlet square) rather than chosen. If the nonabelian Dirichlet
square exists and is computable, that is a genuinely new attack.

**(B) GR in the classical limit.** *Status: essentially done, with
one open.* The action is Palatini GR (0046/0050), so the classical
tests pass by construction (0059/0060). The remaining item is the
construction's expressive power at second order — the bond's h²
(0060) — which is about the web's solution-building, not about the
theory's classical content.

**(C) QFT in curved spacetime in the semiclassical limit.** Hawking
temperature, Unruh effect, horizon entropy — the standard benchmarks
any candidate must reproduce or explain away. *Status: untouched.*
No horizons, no thermality, no matter QFT anywhere in the repo. This
is the largest silent gap. (It may also be the program's best home
turf: horizon entropy as *lost pairwise capacity* is exactly the
kind of statement the information tier is built to make — the
deficit law and mass monogamy are area-law-shaped tools. But nothing
is built.)

**(D) A UV story.** What happens at the Planck scale, from principle
rather than choice. *Status: the regulator exists, its value does
not.* The level N is the natural UV structure, and 0049 proved the
sharp version: κ is a unit, G cancels from the quantized ledger, **N
is the coupling's only physical datum**. So the program will never
"predict G" (a unit); what it must predict is N — equivalently, why
gravity is weak. N is currently one of exactly two knobs (with t,
and 0063 suggests t's tuning dissolves in the continuous theory). A
program that derives N derives the hierarchy.

**(E) Matter.** Gravity alone is not a theory of the world. The web
needs matter fields beyond scripted sources, coupled through
participation. *Status: standing open since 0048; the movie/event
threads are the intended seed and are dormant.*

**(F) The arena.** Two assumptions currently do silent load-bearing
work: the identification of the knowledge manifold with physical
spacetime (0058's crux (c) — the step holography *earns* as a
duality), and the choice of 3+1 Lorentzian. The dimensional trade
(0043, 0061, 0063: 2+1 topological, 3+1 carries Weyl) is suggestive
structure, not a derivation — dimension is chosen. Lorentzian
signature is handled at the criterion level (0063 §3) but the
quantum arena is Euclidean/static throughout. *Status: the program's
deepest unpriced assumptions, unchanged since 0058.*

**(G) The measurement problem, jointly.** Not on the field's
standard list — but it is on *this* program's list, because its
postulates make measurement and geometry one machine. The newest
result gives the concrete form: weight = undecidedness, so
decoherence should be the transfer of weight from capacity to inert
bias — **collapse as geometric discharge** (0068 open 1). No other
program attempts this unification; this one is obligated to.

**The path, in dependency order:**
1. The nonabelian/continuous interacting measure (the wall; the

2. Matter on the web, coupled via participation.
3. Semiclassical benchmarks: Unruh/Hawking/area laws from the

4. Derive N (the hierarchy) — and confirm t's tuning dissolves.
5. The arena: dimension and signature from the postulates; the

A skeptic is convinced at: (A) + (C) + one derived knob. Nothing

## 2. The falsifiability path, vacuum energy first

**The mechanism is already in the theory.** The cosmological
constant mode — spatially uniform curvature — is the zero mode, and
the program deletes it *by constraint*: the closed-universe budget
Σ F = 0 (0029, 0053), which reappeared as the removed k = 0 mode in
quantum Newton (0057) and as the measure's one divergence, Ŵ(0) ∝
P/φ → ∞, in the divisor ensemble (0064). The measure's sole infinity
and the postulates' sole global constraint are the same object. So
the program already has unimodular-gravity phenomenology: **the
uniform part of vacuum energy does not gravitate; only its structure
does.** The 10¹²⁰ problem does not arise in the standard form,
because sourcing tracks recorded relative-coordinate information,
not energy density.

**The falsifiable-shaped continuation.** At finite N the budget is
mod-N: Σ F ≡ 0 (mod N) admits residual total flux in integer
multiples of the level. That is a **quantized global-curvature
spectrum** — a Λ-like residual that comes in units set by N and the
arena's size, rather than a continuum dial. The steps to a real
prediction, each honest about its risk:
1. Reformulate the budget off the compact torus (the constraint was
   derived on closed lattices; an open or expanding arena changes
   it — this is a real conceptual step, and the compactness
   assumption currently does work).
2. Treat the zero mode in the continuum τ-theory (0064 §4's
   delicacy is exactly this).
3. Compute the residual spectrum: the allowed values of total
   curvature × volume, presumably ∝ 2πn/N.
4. Confront: is Λ_obs · (horizon volume) consistent with a small
   integer multiple of the quantum, and what N does that require?
   Cross-check that N against (D)'s hierarchy requirement — the two
   knob-derivations must meet at one value.

Steps 3–4 are speculative until 1–2 exist; but every step is
computable in-model, and the *shape* — Λ quantized, small because N
is large, with N cross-checked against the strength of gravity — is
a genuine falsifiable target, not a hope.

**The other falsifiability lines, with status:** graviton mass
(alive only if the interacting theory renormalizes t; 0063's free
theory is massless with no tuning, so this line may have dissolved —
which is itself the honest finding); the tangle-vs-entropy
discriminator (0067 — sharp but in-model); the per-carrier π cap and
mass monogamy (0066/0068 — theorems, Planck-scale, not near-term
observable); equivalence-principle-type deviations (dead — 0059).
Nothing on this list is a near-term laboratory prediction. The Λ
line is the only credible observational route currently visible.
