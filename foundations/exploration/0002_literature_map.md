| Idea in this theory | Closest established framework | Relationship / what's new |
|---|---|---|
| Perspective of one particle; no absolute state (P1) | **Relational QM**, Rovelli 1996 | Essentially RQM's core claim. Not new; we adopt it. |
| Wavefunction = a system's *belief*, updated optimally (P5, Bayesian fusion) | **QBism**, Fuchs–Mermin–Schack; **epistemic view**, Spekkens toy model 2007 | QBism already reads the state as an agent's Bayesian degree of belief and the Born rule as a coherence norm. Not new. Spekkens shows how far an epistemic-restriction model reproduces QM — and where it *cannot* (Bell). |
| Belief is over *relative* coordinates, from a particle's frame (P1, objects) | **Quantum reference frames**: Aharonov–Kaufherr; Bartlett–Rudolph–Spekkens 2007; **Giacomini–Castro-Ruiz–Brukner 2019** | This is the rigorous home for "wavefunction of B from A's viewpoint." QRF makes superposition and entanglement *frame-dependent* — a genuine, testable structure. Leading candidate for a distinctive prediction. |
| "Could-have-known" collapses the wave; air/apparatus all count (double slit) | **Decoherence / einselection**, Zurek; which-path complementarity | Standard and experimentally solid. The "could have known, not did know" phrasing is exactly environmental monitoring. Not new; correctly invoked. |
| Buckyball stays one molecule; intramolecular relative uncertainty pre-collapsed | Decoherence of internal vs. CoM DOF; matter-wave interferometry (Arndt–Zeilinger line) | Correct qualitative reading. The a-priori tight *relative* coordinate of bound atoms = a sharp `ρ_{internal}` that no environment needs to re-measure. |
| Pairwise + recursive consistency (P3, P4) | **Quantum marginal problem**; monogamy of entanglement/correlations; **data-processing inequality** | Packaging is the contribution: consistency ⇒ marginal representability; recursion ⇒ variance additivity / DPI. Clean statement; the underlying theorems are standard. |
| Split/merge ⇒ sharp relative position, uncertain momentum (P5, mechanism) | **EPR 1935** (used `q1−q2` and `p1+p2`, which commute); SPDC EPR realization, **Howell et al. 2004** | Refinement is a real contribution: it is *relative* momentum that is uncertain; *total* momentum is sharp by conservation. Both `q1−q2` and `p1+p2` can be sharp together. See `mechanism/`. |
| Locality: "acts on what it knows," nothing faster than *c* (P2) | Relativistic causality; no-signalling; **Bell 1964** and loophole-free tests (Hensen/Giustina/Shalm 2015) | This is the locality premise of Bell. It is the source of the fork, not a free win. |

## Net assessment

- **Inherited (do not claim as new):** the relational stance, the epistemic/
  Bayesian reading of the state, decoherence as the mechanism of apparent
  collapse, the QRF formalism for "B from A's frame."
- **Contributed here:** (1) P4 as variance-additivity/DPI on relative
  coordinates; (2) the EPR refinement of the split/merge mechanism; (3) forcing
  the Bell fork as the theory's decisive statement; (4) identifying QRF
  frame-dependence as the one place the picture might still say something
  testably distinctive.
- **Not yet delivered by anyone (including here):** a *unique dynamics* or a
  first-principles Born rule from P1–P5. This is the honest frontier.
