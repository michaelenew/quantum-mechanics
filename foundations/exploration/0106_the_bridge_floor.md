## Honest limits

- The laws compared are the bare stack weights (the exact tier);
  dressed laws (0101) would shift the KL matrix — but dressing is
  level-dependent too, so identifiability survives; the numbers
  would change, the floor's existence would not.
- The universal integer code is one prior among admissible levels;
  any prior with finite differences moves n* by O(Δprior/KL) — a few
  samples.
